### 👥 [Mall Customer Segmentation — Clustering Analysis](https://github.com/mwmnmhsn/customer-segmentation-clustering)
> Python · scikit-learn · Pandas · Matplotlib · Seaborn · SciPy

Statistics graduation project comparing 3 clustering algorithms — K-Means, Hierarchical (Agglomerative), and DBSCAN — on 200 mall customers.

- Used the Elbow Method + Silhouette Score to determine K=5; **K-Means: 0.5547 · Hierarchical: 0.5538 · DBSCAN: 0.5849**
- Ran DBSCAN hyperparameter grid search (eps × min_samples), auto-flagging 45 outlier customers (22.5% of data)
- Translated results into 5 business segments (VIP, Standard, At-Risk, Potential Premium, Casual Shoppers) with tailored marketing strategies, delivered as a Business Segmentation Bubble Map
- Generated 19 visualizations and 14 structured CSV reports, including PCA comparisons and a full model comparison (Silhouette, Davies-Bouldin, Calinski-Harabasz)
